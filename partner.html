About Us Partner Privacy Terms Disclaimer HIPAA

Pragma provides nutrition education and decision support. Pragma is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease. Recommendations are educational and authored by Registered Dietitians. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, managing a medical condition, or reviewing lab results.

We do not sell your personal data. Any clinical review uses de-identified, aggregated information where appropriate.
