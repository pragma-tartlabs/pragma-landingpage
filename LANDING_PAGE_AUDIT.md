# Pragma Landing Page Audit
**Compare: Current index.html vs. PRAGMA_PHASE1_PLAN.md**

---

## Executive Summary

The landing page does an excellent job of explaining Pragma's *value proposition* and *differentiation* but **significantly underframes the founding cohort recruitment narrative**. It reads more like a product launch page than a "first 1,000 women" founding member campaign. The page is visually strong and the trust elements are present, but they're scattered and lack the urgency and clarity needed to drive conversion for a limited cohort offer.

**Key issue:** Visitors learn *what* Pragma does, but not *why they should join now* as part of this specific, limited-time founding cohort.

## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section ✅ STRONG
**Current:** "Free for the first 1,000 women" kicker + "You've been told you're fine. You know you're not."

**Strengths:**
- Kicker immediately signals limited-time offer and cohort size
- Headline resonates with target persona (women who know something is wrong)
- Hero body nails the RD-authored + zero brand deals positioning

**Gaps:**
- Doesn't explain *why* "first 1,000" matters (other than scarcity)
- Doesn't say: "Be part of the founding cohort" or "Help us train the algorithm"
- No sense of *mission* beyond "get your protocol"

**Plan alignment:** ⚠️ Hero hits the surface level but misses the cohort narrative.

### 2. Social Proof (4-item list) ✅ STRONG
**Current:** RD-authored · Zero affiliate revenue · HIPAA-compliant · Brand-agnostic

**Strengths:**
- Covers all 4 trust pillars from the plan
- Order is smart: clinical (RD) → commercial (no affiliate) → security (HIPAA) → neutrality (brand-agnostic)
- Concise and scannable

**Gaps:**
- "Zero affiliate revenue. Ever." is strong but doesn't explain *what this means*
- "Brand-agnostic by design" needs elaboration: "We rank supplements on evidence, not profit"
- HIPAA mentioned 3x on page but no detail on data protection (encryption, BAA, etc.)
- No mention of the specific audit trail: "Every recommendation is traceable to Whitney's clinical rules + sign-off date"

**Plan alignment:** ✅ Trust elements are present, but lack depth.

### 6. "How It Works" Section ⚠️ NEEDS REFOCUS
**Current:** 3 steps (Intake 10–15 min · Foundation Stack Day 1 · Daily check-in + monthly reviews)

**Strengths:**
- Covers all 3 Phase 1 milestones (intake, protocol output, tracker)
- Mentions progressive unlock: "starter protocol after 2 modules"
- Mentions monthly phase reviews and Phase 2 unlocks
- Step 3 hints at clean attribution ("If something helps, you'll know it was that")

**Critical gaps:**
1. **Step 1 downplays the progressive unlock**
   - Says "Six intake modules" but doesn't emphasize: "You get a full protocol after just 2 modules"
   - Doesn't explain that modules 3–6 are optional but make it "sharper"
   - Doesn't say: "No all-or-nothing gate" (from the plan)

2. **Step 2 doesn't explain the Foundation Stack clearly**
   - Says "3–6 supplements with dose, form, timing, rationale" — good
   - Says "traceable to a specific clinical rule Whitney wrote" — good
   - Doesn't explain: **What makes the Foundation Stack special in Phase 1?**
   - Missing: "This is your personalized baseline, not a generic recommendation"

3. **Step 3 is the biggest gap — doesn't explain daily check-ins' PURPOSE**
   - Mentions "Energy, sleep, mood, adherence — logged in under a minute" — good
   - Mentions "Monthly phase reviews unlock Phase 2 supplements one at a time, every 30 days" — good
   - **MISSING:** Why check-ins matter to the user
   - **MISSING:** "Your daily data trains the recommendation engine — you're helping build Pragma"
   - **MISSING:** Streak mechanics, nudges, consistency incentives
   - **MISSING:** "One variable means clean attribution" explanation is vague

4. **Doesn't frame this as the cohort's journey**
   - Reads like a product feature list, not: "Here's what being a founding member looks like"
   - No sense of: "You're not just a user — you're a data partner helping us refine the algorithm"

**Plan alignment:** ⚠️ Content is technically correct but *misses the emotional/narrative frame* that distinguishes Phase 1 recruitment from regular product marketing.

---

### 7. Pricing Section ⚠️ NEEDS COHORT CONTEXT
**Current:** 4 tiers (Free $0 · Pragma+ $8/mo · Pragma Pro $22/mo · Pragma Report $199 once)

**Strengths:**
- Clear tier structure
- Free tier is featured
- Pragma+ is marked "Most popular"
- Pro tier shows future Phase 2 features
- Report tier offers one-time buy option

**Critical gaps:**
1. **Free tier description is misleading for Phase 1 users**
   - Says: "The full Foundation Stack protocol + community access"
   - Doesn't say: "During Phase 1 (the first ~4 months), this is completely free for the first 1,000 women"
   - Says: "Brand picks with affiliate links" — but Phase 1 Plan says "No affiliate links in Phase 1"
   - **Contradiction with trust pillar**

2. **Doesn't explain the Phase 1 → Phase 2 transition**
   - Doesn't say: "At the end of Phase 1, you'll choose: stay on Free (limited features), upgrade to Pragma+, or cancel"
   - No urgency: "After the free phase, Pragma+ is $8/mo"
   - No explanation of what "free phase" duration is

3. **Pro and Report tiers feel out of scope**
   - These are Phase 2 features (labs, genetics, wearables)
   - For Phase 1 recruitment, showing them creates confusion: "Wait, is this free or not?"
   - Better to remove them or put them in a "Phase 2 preview" section

4. **Doesn't frame Free tier as a *ask* from the user**
   - The plan asks Phase 1 users to: do intake, check in daily, enable Pragma to train
   - Pricing section should say: "Free because we need your data to train the algorithm"
   - Current framing: "Free because you're lucky" (passive)

**Plan alignment:** ❌ Doesn't explain the cohort economics. Pricing reads like a mature SaaS product, not a founding member offer.

3. **Doesn't reinforce the team effort**

## Missing Sections

### 1. Founding Cohort Narrative
**What's needed:** A section that explicitly explains:
- "You are not a user. You are a founding member."
- "Your daily check-ins train our recommendation engine — your input directly shapes what we build next."
- "For Phase 1, you get full access free. After Phase 1, you can stay on the free tier (limited) or upgrade to Pragma+ ($8/mo)."
- "The first 1,000 women will have helped us build something that works."
- **Suggested placement:** After "How it works" or before "Pricing"

### 2. The Data Story
**What's needed:** Explain what happens with their daily check-in data:
- "We analyze your patterns to refine the recommendation engine."
- "Every Phase 2 supplement you unlock is because your data (and others') revealed a need."
- "Clean attribution: if something works for you, we track what caused it."
- **Suggested placement:** As subsection of "How it works" or separate "Your data matters" section

### 3. Waitlist Mechanics
**What's needed:** (From the plan)
- "Position in queue" tracking
- "Referral loop: refer 3 friends, move up 50 spots"
- "Confirmation email shows your position"
- **Suggested placement:** After signup, or in a separate benefits callout

### 4. Phase 1 Timeline
**What's needed:**
- When does Phase 1 start/end?
- When does Phase 2 unlock?
- When does the free period end?
- **Suggested placement:** Could be subtle (fine print) or explicit (new timeline section)

### 5. Trust & Compliance Details
**What's needed:**
- "HIPAA-compliant infrastructure: data encrypted at rest and in transit"
- "BAA in place before any health inputs collected"
- "Pragma never sells or shares your health data"
- **Suggested placement:** Could expand footer or create a "Your privacy" section

## Conclusion

The landing page is visually excellent and hits most key messages. However, **it's missing the cohort narrative that makes Phase 1 recruitment different from standard SaaS marketing.** Visitors understand *what* Pragma does but not *why they should join now as a founding member* or *how their participation matters.*

The primary edit required is **narrative reframing**: change "sign up for a free supplement protocol" to "become a founding member who trains the algorithm." The rest is refinement.

Most sections can stay as-is if this cohort positioning is threaded through Steps 3 and the final CTA.
