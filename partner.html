Partnership

We partner with people who don't cut corners.

Two kinds of partners make Pragma better: clinicians who bring expertise into the community, and supplement brands whose products actually meet the bar. Neither gets special treatment. Both get something worth having.

Who are you?

For healthcare professionals

Your expertise belongs in this conversation.

Pragma's community is full of women asking exactly the kinds of questions you field every day, about labs, hormones, life stage, and what's actually worth taking. Verified HCPs can engage with their credentials visible, contribute to clinical discussions, and help us make the guidance better. No brand deals, no commissions, no noise.

🏷️
A badge that means something

Verified HCPs are identified in the community by credential and specialty. Not a username. When you respond, users know they're hearing from a licensed professional in your field. We verify manually. We don't hand out badges.

🔬
Evidence-first, always

Pragma's protocol is RD-authored and evidence-graded. When you contribute, you're engaging with a community that already understands mechanism-first thinking. You're not starting from scratch, you're deepening a conversation that's already happening at a high level.

🔒
Your license is yours

Verification requires your license number and state. We check it, we confirm it, and then we keep it private. It never appears publicly. What appears publicly is your credential, specialty, and optionally your institution and a short bio.

📣
Reach women who are already asking

The women in our community are self-educated, lab-curious, and asking better questions than most providers expect. They don't need to be convinced nutrition matters, they need trustworthy clinical voices to help them navigate it.

What HCP partnership looks like

1
Submit your credentials

Designation, specialty, training, current practice, license number and state. We also welcome a professional website, it helps us verify faster. Takes about five minutes.

2
Manual verification

Our team checks your license against your state medical or nutrition board. We aim to verify within 2–3 business days. You'll hear from us either way.

3
Community access with credentials visible

Once verified, your replies in the community display your credential and specialty. Users always know they're hearing from a licensed professional. Transparency is the feature, it's what makes your voice worth hearing here.

4
Deeper collaboration, if it fits

Some HCPs become Clinical Advisory Board contributors. Some co-author protocol notes or contribute to our evidence review process. That conversation happens after we know each other. It starts with verification.

What we never do

  • Pay you to recommend specific products or brands
  • Ask you to promote anything. Your role here is clinical, not marketing
  • Publish your license number or personal contact information
  • Misrepresent your affiliation, you choose what appears next to your name

For supplement brands

If your formula is good, we want to know about it.

Pragma evaluates every supplement on the same rubric: dose, form, evidence, sourcing, and manufacturing quality. Combination products often can't be fully evaluated from a label alone. If you're willing to share your full formula under NDA, we can evaluate it properly, and recommend it to the women it's actually right for.

🔐
Your formula stays yours

Proprietary doses are covered under a mutual NDA and stored encrypted. They are accessed only by Pragma's clinical team and the recommendation engine. Never surfaced to users, never shared with competitors or third parties.

✍️
You write what users see

Your user-facing description is written and approved by you. We use it verbatim. Users see your brand name, your approved description, and our qualitative assessment. Never your exact doses or proprietary ratios.

📦
Consolidation is the pitch

When your combination product meets clinical doses for ingredients we'd otherwise recommend separately, we surface the consolidation to the user, along with the cost savings. That's a meaningful recommendation. A single product that replaces three is a real outcome for the woman taking it.

⚖️
Same rubric as everyone else

Disclosure doesn't inflate your ranking. Your SKU is evaluated on the same clinical criteria as every single-ingredient product we recommend. If a dose is below threshold, users see that. We don't suppress findings because a brand is in our network.

How the process works

1
Brand Disclosure Brief

We send you a one-page overview of the process, the NDA terms, and what we're asking for. No commitment required at this stage.

2
Mutual NDA

Standard mutual agreement. Covers confidentiality of your formula and Pragma's evaluation rubric. Five-year term, Delaware jurisdiction. Signed before anything is shared.

3
Full formula disclosure

Ingredients, doses, forms, sourcing, third-party testing, contraindications, and your approved user-facing description. One form per SKU, re-submitted on any reformulation.

4
RD evaluation

Our clinical team evaluates your disclosure against our protocol rubric. You receive the qualitative output before it goes live, so there are no surprises.

5
Live in the engine

Your SKU becomes eligible for recommendations to users it's actually right for. You're notified when it goes live and when it's first recommended.

What we never do

  • Share your proprietary formula with any third party, ever
  • Rank your product higher because you disclosed, quality is the only input
  • Accept changes to your user-facing description that misrepresent the formula
  • Recommend your product to users it isn't appropriate for, regardless of relationship
  • Take payment to influence where your product ranks

Not ready to disclose?

  • You don't have to. Pragma evaluates every product on publicly available label data where possible.
  • Products without NDA disclosure are labeled "Proprietary blend, doses not disclosed" and are excluded from consolidation recommendations.
  • Disclosure is always available later. When you're ready, the process starts with a conversation.
