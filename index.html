Why Pragma exists

Women are told they're fine. Generic supplement advice ignores life stage, hormones, medications, and individual needs. Pragma exists to bring clarity, evidence, and personalization to supplementation.

Your life stage is a first-class input.

Not a footnote. Not a modifier. Your body isn't static. Your nutrition recommendations shouldn't be either.

GLP-1 users

On semaglutide or tirzepatide?

GLP-1 drugs reduce appetite and accelerate nutrient depletion. Lean mass, bone density, B12, iron. The gaps compound quickly. Pragma builds your protocol around the specific depletion pattern.

On birth control

Years on the pill?

Combined contraceptives deplete B6, B12, folate, magnesium, zinc, and CoQ10 over time. Almost no prescriber mentions it. Pragma does, with a specific protocol to address it.

Postpartum

After pregnancy or nursing?

Your body ran a marathon for nine months. Ferritin, iodine, omega-3, choline. The recovery list is specific, and the stakes are real. Built from your labs, not a generic checklist.

Perimenopause

Sleep, brain fog, mood shifts?

Progesterone drops first. Estrogen clearance shifts. Magnesium, creatine, and targeted B-complex have real RCT evidence for what you're experiencing. Let's sequence them correctly.

Questions

Good questions. Here are the honest answers.

What exactly is Pragma?

Pragma is a nutrition guidance app for women navigating specific life stages: GLP-1 use, perimenopause, hormonal birth control, and postpartum recovery. We help you understand which supplements actually matter for your body right now, and which ones are just noise.

Do you sell supplements?

No, and that's intentional. We never sell our own supplements. We evaluate products already on the market based on ingredients, dose, form, and evidence, then tell you what's worth it.

What's a Registered Dietitian?

A Registered Dietitian (RD) is a licensed healthcare professional with a clinical nutrition degree, supervised practice, and ongoing continuing education. Pragma was built by two RDs, Whitney Brooke Isola and Samantha Peebles, and every recommendation is built on that foundation.

What if you earn a commission from a link?

If a product we recommend has an affiliate program, we use it, and we always label it. But the affiliate link comes after the recommendation, never before. Rankings are based purely on evidence. Transparent commissions are how we keep the free tier free.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pragma provides nutrition education and decision support. It's not a substitute for your doctor or care team. Always loop in your clinician, especially around medications, labs, pregnancy, or breastfeeding.

When is Pragma launching?

We're in early access. Join the list and you'll be among the first to try it. You'll also get our newsletter with evidence-based nutrition insights in the meantime.

What happens to my data?

We use your data to make Pragma more useful for you, not to sell you more things. The information you share helps us personalize your recommendations around your life stage, preferences, and goals. We don't sell your data, and we'll always aim to be clear about what we collect, why we collect it, and how it's used.

We're also building Pragma with strong privacy and data practices from the start, including a HIPAA-compliant setup where appropriate. When healthcare professionals review patterns to help improve the engine, they only look at de-identified, aggregated trends, not individual profiles. That helps us make Pragma smarter and more evidence-based while protecting the privacy of the women using it.
