Foundation Stack Foundation Stack & 30 day plan

Fine-tuning, together Targeted & community support

As your check-ins (and any labs/tests you choose to share) come in, we refine your stack one variable at a time. And you're never doing this alone: a community of women navigating the same stuff is right there with you, sharing what's working in real time.

Staying with you Monthly reviews compound results

Daily check-ins, monthly reviews, and personalized questions for your next provider visit, so your stack keeps evolving with you, and you walk into appointments equipped, not guessing.

"One variable at a time" is how you know which supplement is working. Pragma sequences on purpose. Daily tracking builds more accurate health picture over time to finetune recommendations. Labs and provider conversations are suggestions, not diagnoses, and tests are never run in-house. Pragma is nutrition education, not medical advice.
